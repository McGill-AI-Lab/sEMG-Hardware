A · Shorted-input noise floor

– SKIPclamshell_validation.md §2

Measure the intrinsic noise of the ADS1298 as mounted on your board, with the inputs shorted inside the chip. Every other experiment is compared against this baseline.

CHnSET MUX = 001 (input shorted), internal 2.4 V reference, HR mode. Input-referred µVrms, µVpp, pp/rms and offset per channel, compared with the datasheet noise level.

– SKIPclamshell_validation.md §3

Find what KIND of noise is present: flat white floor, 1/f rise, mains lines, USB or ESP32 lines.

Welch PSD (Hann window, ≥ 8 averaged segments) of Experiment A's captures, shown as amplitude spectral density. Lines more than 10 dB above the local floor are listed with a likely cause.

C · Gain sweep (PGA vs ADC noise)

– SKIPclamshell_validation.md §4

Find which block dominates the noise. Input-referred noise should fall with gain and flatten out: the flat level is the PGA noise, the low-gain rise is ADC noise divided by the gain.

Shorted inputs at every gain; fit v_in² = e_PGA² + (e_ADC/G)² per channel.

D · Data-rate sweep

– SKIPclamshell_validation.md §5

Check that the noise behaves as white noise (RMS grows as √bandwidth, ×√2 per doubling) and separate real in-band lines (fixed frequency) from aliases (move when the rate changes).

Re-uses Experiment A's captures at every data rate.

E · Multichannel correlation

– SKIPclamshell_validation.md §6

Separate noise that belongs to each channel from noise that all channels share (reference, supply, ground). Shared noise looks like a real synchronous signal and is the most dangerous kind for sEMG.

Experiment A's all-channel capture, high-pass filtered at 1 Hz: Pearson correlation matrix, RMS of the channel average vs σ/√N, and the mean coherence spectrum over all channel pairs.

  • – SKIPNeeds an Experiment A capture with channel_plan 'all'.

F · Aggressor toggling (ESP32, USB, SPI)

– SKIPclamshell_validation.md §7

Find which system activity couples into the ADC by changing one thing at a time against a baseline.

All channels shorted; each variant changes one of SPI clock, CPU load, WiFi mode, or USB activity (buffering in RAM). The first variant is the baseline.

  • – SKIPNo captures.

G · Internal test signal

– SKIPclamshell_validation.md §8

Check gain, scaling, offset, timing and reference noise with the chip's own square wave. Also tests the known 'amplitude ≈ 1/6 of expected' problem with a gain cross-check.

MUX = 101 (test signal), INT_TEST = 1. Square wave: amplitude, midpoint, plateau noise, period. Gain cross-check: input-referred amplitude must not depend on gain. DC levels at both amplitudes: plateau noise vs shorted noise reveals reference noise.

MUX = 011 (MVDD), gain 1. Channels 1, 2, 5–8 measure (AVDD + AVSS)/2; channels 3 and 4 measure DVDD/4. Repeated under each listed variant (e.g. WiFi transmitting).
