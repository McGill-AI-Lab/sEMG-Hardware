# Input-block component evidence

27 October 2026. Input builder only; no active schematic edit. Native [candidate netlist](input-blocks-netlist.xml) matches the physical pin contract in [input-blocks-pin-contract.json](input-blocks-pin-contract.json). Candidate ERC has only two singleton labels that the complete design connects and an unregistered custom library warning; final integration must resolve those honestly.

- **J17 JST B2B-PH-K-S(LF)(SN):** [manufacturer PH catalog](https://www.jst-mfg.com/product/pdf/eng/ePH.pdf) specifies 2 A with AWG24, 2.0 mm pitch. Use PHR-2 housing and SPH-002T-P0.5S crimp contacts with appropriate wire/crimp process. The schematic deliberately assigns pin1 positive/pin2 return; verify actual pack harness polarity because a two-pin connector family does not define battery polarity universally.
- **F1 Littelfuse 046601.5NR:** [manufacturer 466 datasheet, pp.1–3](https://www.littelfuse.com/assetdocs/littelfuse_fuse_466_datasheet?assetguid=dbe9bcd7-6072-4adf-bf5b-d33e52a6b90f): 1.5 A, 63 V, 1206, 50 A interrupt rating, 0.046 Ω nominal cold resistance and 0.1103 A²s nominal melting integral. The required 25% continuous-current rerating reduces 1.5 A to 1.125 A at room conditions; its 70 °C example adds a 0.8 temperature factor, giving 0.9 A. Thus approximately 1 A is a pulse target at that temperature, not an approved continuous load. Check startup I²t, repetitive RF pulses, prospective pack short current and pack-protection coordination. F1 is secondary board protection, not a wearer-current limiter.
- **Q1 Alpha & Omega AO3401A:** [manufacturer datasheet](https://www.aosmd.com/res/data_sheets/AO3401A.pdf) confirms SOT-23 physical 1=gate/2=source/3=drain; VDS −30 V, VGS ±12 V and RDS(on) ≤85 mΩ at VGS −2.5 V and the stated 25 °C condition. The flattened installed symbol retains those pin numbers. For reverse-polarity protection, D3 is the fused input and S2 the protected positive output; G1 has R52=100 kΩ to AGND. Its initial body-diode conduction develops negative gate-source bias for channel turn-on. This is **not reverse-current blocking**: externally powered downstream rails can drive upstream through its enabled channel.
- **JP1 Samtec TSW-102-07-G-S:** [manufacturer product page](https://www.samtec.com/products/tsw-102-07-g-s) confirms two positions, single row, 2.54 mm through-hole header. Fit without shunt for normal operation; short only to inhibit MAIN_ENABLE. Battery unplug remains OFF; EN inhibition is not source isolation.
- **C57 Panasonic 6SVP220M:** [manufacturer product specification](https://industrial.panasonic.com/ww/products/pt/os-con/models/6SVP220M) confirms 220 µF ±20%, 6.3 V, 25 mΩ ESR at 100 kHz, 3.7 Arms ripple at 100 kHz/105 °C, 277 µA maximum leakage and size F8 (10.0 mm diameter ×7.9 mm body length). Installed KiCad `Capacitor_SMD:CP_Elec_10x7.9` explicitly describes Panasonic F8. Use polarized positive at MAIN and negative AGND; verify land pattern against current [Panasonic mounting guidance](https://industrial.panasonic.com/cdbs/www-data/pdf/AAB8000/AAB8000COL10.pdf). Full effective output capacitance spans at least 176–264 µF for this part alone; include converter ceramics and DevKit capacitance when checking startup/stability/transients.

At 1 A and 25 °C, nominal fuse resistance plus the stated MOSFET RDS bound gives approximately 0.131 V path drop and 0.131 W dissipation, excluding connectors/wiring and fuse heating. At 0.8 A, the corresponding screen is approximately 105 mV and 84 mW. Hot MOSFET resistance, fuse resistance/derating and connector/cable loss must be included in battery cutoff and RF transient qualification. No actual battery part or measured fault-clearing behavior is established by these component selections.
