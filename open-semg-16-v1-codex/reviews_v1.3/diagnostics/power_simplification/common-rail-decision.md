# Common-rail minimal ADC decision

27 October2026. This follows the user's preference for fewer parts and supersedes the switched-ADC option in `adc-minimal.md` for the present implementation decision. No design edits by this reviewer.

**A load switch is not fundamentally required by the ADS1298 datasheet.** A single3.3 V main rail powering MCU, ADC DVDD and the digital buffers, with a short2.2 Ω filtered AVDD/AVDD1 branch, is a reasonable minimum bench architecture. One always-enabled SN74LVC244A provides defined forward logic levels. One MAIN-powered74LVC2G125 regenerates the shared MISO and U1DRDY return signals. Separate ADC CS/shared MISO, shared clock and all16 acquisition channels remain intact. No extra ADC-power GPIO, second regulator, mux, readiness gate or supervisor tree is required solely by the ADS startup text.

The required condition is an observed startup consistent with TI's low-input guidance, rather than a mandated block type:

- Move the existing five10 kΩ control pulldowns to the MCU-side244 inputs and add10 kΩ on both MCU-side CS inputs. The244's input leakage is at most5 μA through85°C,20 μA through125°C; even the latter plus the ESP50 nA benchmark across10.1 kΩ is approximately0.203 V, below the244's0.8 V low-input limit. The MCU must not retain a GPIO pullup/hold or drive conflicting states through startup.
- Keep the244 OEs grounded, supply it fromMAIN, and leave its ADC outputs free of external DC pulldowns. Two ADS inputs contribute at most20 μA under their stated powered conditions. The244's light-load output specification therefore gives VOH≥VCC−0.2 V through85°C and VOL≤0.2 V, with roughly0.45 V ADS high margin at3.25 V. Through125°C the244 table instead uses VCC−0.3 V. These bounds must not be mixed between grades. ESP→244 has a much less demanding TTL input threshold than direct ESP→ADS; check the actual0.33 mA MCU pulldown load in bench measurements.
- ESP32-S3 GPIO6–14 have no default WPU/WPD listed and have documented LOW-only power-up glitches; the external pulldowns establish low when undriven. Firmware boots by driving all seven signals LOW, allowing rails to settle, then deselecting both ADCs and running the full existing reset/clock/reference initialization. Forbid retained GPIO/RTC hold. Repeat initialization on reset; no unverified bootloader behavior is assumed to configure the ADC.
- Retain3.6 kΩ,1% per DOUT branch. The possible opposed-driver current at3.35 V is≤0.470 mA, below the±500 μA characterized output load. This bounds the initial both-CS-low state without changing normal cascade operation. It does not authorize simultaneous CS selection during transactions, and the ADC input-current10 mA limit is not a DOUT rating.

With a2.2 Ω analog filter and20.2 μF effective capacitance, τ≈44.4 μs and the nominal pole is3.58 kHz. Added pin bypasses increaseτ somewhat. Typical two-ADC5.5 mA analog load drops approximately12.1 mV; a10 mA analog allocation drops22 mV, far less than the former2.98 V rail reduction. Use actual current, resistor tolerance and capacitor effective values for the minimum AVDD. Auxiliary17/18/62 follow filtered actualAVDD; U1CLKSEL remains onDVDD/MAIN. All grounds remain bonded.

For a ramp, analog lag is approximatelyτ×MAIN slew. Keeping that estimated lag below2.1 V withτ44.4 μs calls for slew below47 mV/μs; this is compatible with a reasonably slow converter startup, subject to its actual capacitance/load/ramp. Measure MAIN andAVDD; do not label the brief RC lag a demonstrated failure or claim the arithmetic is a startup waveform. Wait for analog settling before releasing reset/powerdown. Analog-input startup still uses dummy inputs; F07/F08 are not closed.

The MAIN-powered return buffer has explicit Ioff and TTL inputs, and both OEs may be grounded. Put the6.8 kΩ series resistors between its outputs and MCU pins to limit a documented MCU LOW glitch against a high buffer output to approximately0.5 mA. The shared MISO resistor junction feeds one return-buffer input; U1DRDY feeds the other. This retains the logical shared bus and protects the direct driver conflict without extra gating.

**Two separate RC nodes affect SPI timing.** An illustrative20 pF DOUT-bus node gives approximately85 ns threshold delay through3.6 kΩ into the return buffer's2.0 V input. A separate20 pF MCU-side node gives approximately202 ns through6.8 kΩ to the ESP0.75×VDD input threshold. Add17 ns ADC delay and buffer/forward delays: total is approximately315–320 ns, exceeding the250 ns half-cycle at2 MHz. With the first node20 pF, the MCU-side capacitance needs to be roughly12 pF or less to leave useful setup margin at2 MHz. Do not call2 MHz/4 kSPS guaranteed from a single20 pF model. Start at1 MHz and≤2 kSPS, then verify2 MHz with actual loading, edges and any deliberately configured sampling delay. A20 pF probe can itself change this result; use suitable probing.

Primary evidence: local ADS1298 SBAS459K pp.12,16–17,96–97; [TI244 Rev.AG §5.5](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/sn74lvc244a.pdf); [Nexperia2G125 Rev.19](https://assets.nexperia.com/documents/data-sheet/74LVC2G125.pdf); [ESP32-S3 v2.2 Tables2-1/2-2](https://documentation.espressif.com/esp32-s3_datasheet_en.pdf). Remaining measurements are specific bring-up conditions, not reasons to restore the abandoned complex power tree.
