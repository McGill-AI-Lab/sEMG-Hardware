# V1.4 ADC implementation evidence

28 October 2026. Focused static check against the actual starting V1.4 schematic (133 physical symbols), original F01–F21 findings, V1.2 implementation/ERC correction records and the V1.3 focused ADC/VCAP3 decisions. This note specifies the intended simplified implementation; the final native netlist is the evidence for the applied connections.

Primary source: TI [ADS1298 SBAS459K](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/ads1298.pdf), also supplied locally under `docs/datasheets/originals/components/ADS1298.pdf`, printed pp.10–12,16–17,31–33,46,50,56–60,67–69,80–83,85,96–97. Common 3.3V satisfies AVDD–AVSS2.7–5.25V, DVDD1.65–3.6V and allowed AVDD–DVDD difference (p.12). This preserves the F01–F03 corrections without a new regulator or translator.

Retain per ADC VCAP1 pin28=22µF, VCAP2 pin30=1µF, VCAP3 pin55=1µF, VCAP4 pin26=1µF, each returned locally to AVSS/GND. C25/C51 retain **≥10V** ratings and effective-capacitance qualification at about5.2V: VCAP3 is internally generated AVDD+1.9V. This is a nominal projection, not a guaranteed maximum/overshoot bound. Figure106 additionally uses100nF parallel with VCAP3; adding it follows TI's example, while the pin table itself specifies1µF. Do not connect VCAP nodes together or load them as power outputs.

Preserve separate VREFP24 networks C19/C20 and C45/C46=100nF∥10µF to their VREFN/GND returns. Firmware must set CONFIG3.PD_REFBUF=1 and VREF_4V=0 independently on each ADC, and allow reference settling. The4V reference selection requires a5V analog supply and is invalid here.

Retain AVDD/DVDD local bypass banks C6/C7/C8/C9 and C32/C33/C34/C35. Their existing10µF bulk plus100nF values are not grounds for deleting them. TI§11 specifies1µF+100nF bypass and star routing of AVDD1/AVSS1 to the analog supply/return; selected bulk parts must maintain adequate effective capacitance. A local AVDD1 pair is justified by that charge-pump current loop if its physical routing cannot use the existing pair. Figure106 shows AVDD/AVDD1 sharing one bypass pair: an additional pair is not evidence of a confirmed missing-circuit failure. Wi-Fi return currents must not cross the ADC analog/reference return paths; no separate analog regulator is implied.

**SPI and synchronization (F06/F09/F18).** TI§9.4.2.1 explicitly supports shared DIN/SCLK/DOUT with separate CS. DOUT is high impedance with CS high (§9.5.1.4). Use one CS at a time and rail-tracking pullups to deselect both during MCU reset; no external MISO gate is required for that topology. Keep existing10k pulldowns R33–R37 on START/RESET/PWDN/DIN/SCLK. U1CLKSEL52=3V3; U2CLKSEL52=GND; both CLK37 share one net and START38 shares one net. U1CONFIG1.CLK_EN must be1 to export its internal clock; reset clears that bit. At nominal2.048MHz, HR=1/DR=100 gives2kSPS (LP=0/DR=011 also gives2kSPS). Read two complete216-bit frames per sampling epoch. At2MHz this takes216µs of payload time inside500µs; reserve extra time for CS, interrupts and the required four-tCLK end-of-transaction delay. Command decoding needs its own four-tCLK spacing; arbitrary faster SPI command bursts are not automatically valid.

**Startup limitation.** TI§11.1 requests low analog/digital inputs through supply stabilization. CS pullups deliberately provide inactive shared-DOUT defaults; they do not prove literal compliance with the all-inputs-low sentence during a ramp. Keeping RESET/PWDN low is not documented DOUT isolation. Measure startup/reset/brownout behavior and, if needed, obtain TI clarification before declaring sequencing qualified; no unrequested gates/supervisors are justified by an unmeasured contention hypothesis. Once supply and valid clock exist, wait≥2^18 clocks (128ms nominal) and until VCAP1>1.1V, whichever is later; reset low≥2clocks, then wait18clocks. Initialize U1 and enable its clock, then allow U2's clock-dependent POR and individually selected RESET opcode. A subsequent shared hardware RESET or PWDN cycle clears the master's clock configuration and requires the sequence again. Enable/settle references, configure/read back both devices, then issue shared START. Reject missing/misaligned DRDY epochs in firmware.

**Direct logic margins.** TI p.16 specifies VOH≥DVDD−0.4V at−500µA, VOL≤0.4V at500µA; ADS→ESP at3.3V has0.425V nominal table-bound high/low margins. ESP minimum VOH=0.8VDD equals ADS VIH(min)=0.8DVDD, so MCU→ADC has **zero guaranteed high margin** even before accounting for the10k pull load (~0.33mA). Espressif's VOH/VOL table uses high-impedance loading. This limits what static tables prove; scope loaded levels/edges over the bench operating conditions. It does not establish a measured failure or require adding a translator under the common-rail bench scope.

**Analog bench limits (F07/F08/F10/F12/F14).** Preserve all32 series2kΩ and620pF shunt input legs and their matching/rating requirements. They do not clamp an arbitrary simulator voltage or make a human interface safe. For normal acquisition, each input must remain within supply limits and satisfy PGA headroom: `0.2 + G·|VD|/2 < VCM < AVDD−0.2−G·|VD|/2`; differential full scale is±2.4V/G. At3.3V,G=6,VD=0.4V this gives strict VCM1.4–1.9V. A simulator centered near1.65V with a suitably small differential signal is appropriate; do not treat two ground-referenced inputs as guaranteed linear PGA operation. Begin with the internal input-short/test mux and disconnected external sources, then verify simulator DC/common mode before attachment; never drive unpowered ADC inputs. No body-bias or wearer protection is implemented or qualified.
