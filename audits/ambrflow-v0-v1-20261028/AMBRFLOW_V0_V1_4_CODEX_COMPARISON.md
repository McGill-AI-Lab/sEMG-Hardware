# Ambrflow comparison — V0 versus V1.4-codex

**28 October 2026 · read-only electrical audit · no schematic modifications**

**The six previously confirmed V0 wiring/control defects are corrected in the V1.4 schematic.** None of those six wiring defects remains; no new failure covered by this checklist is confirmed. Loaded power, firmware execution and actual analog input behavior remain unverified. A corrected connection is not proof of working acquisition.

This report applies the same 48 original conditions to the two complete active schematics. Ambrflow supplied generic failure conditions, not a component-specific fault list. Column 1 identifies relevant components, why each condition matters and whether it was established in V0. Column 2 explains the V1.4 result, any supported correction and verification. Backups, review candidates and historical snapshots were not used as active sources.

## Verified corrections to the six V0 defects

| V0 problem, affected components, and why it matters | Solved in V1.4? What changed and what remains to check |
|---|---|
| **H2 — Missing ADC analog source.** U1/U2 AVDD19/21/22/54/56/59 joined source-free `+3.3VA`, so there was no valid deliberate analog supply. | **Yes, wiring fixed.** Analog/digital positives reach U3’s 3V3 headers 1/2 on `/+3.3V`; negatives reach `/GND`. Source is the USB-powered complete board. Confirm installed board and loaded rail. A1/F1 did not establish a numerical floating V0 voltage, so are not separately credited as FIXED. |
| **E5 — Paralleled LDO outputs.** U4.5 LP2985 and U7.5 TLV755 joined one rail without defined current sharing. | **Yes.** Both external regulators and battery removed; one board LDO supplies the carrier. Two 3V3 headers from that one output are one source. Keep the documented USB-only source arrangement; no added mux needed. |
| **E6/J3 — Floating LDO enables.** `/EN` contained only U4.3/U7.3, two inputs; startup was not guaranteed. | **Yes.** Regulators and `/EN` removed. Complete U3 board already contains its own EN pull-up/reset network. Its unused external CHIP_PU header is not a missing internal pull-up. |
| **J3 — Intended low straps were not grounded.** RESV1.31, GPIO42/44/45/46 and U2 DAISY_IN41 used isolated `DGND`. | **Yes.** Those low ties now reach real `/GND` with ADC DGND33/49/51 and U3 grounds. Keep unused ADS GPIOs as inputs/low outputs and conflicting optional modes off. |
| **J2/J3 — No hardware ADC reset bias.** PWDN35/RESET36/START38 depended on unbiassed ESP GPIOs before firmware. | **Yes, missing bias fixed.** R35/R34/R33=10 kΩ pull shared controls low. R36/R37 also bias MOSI/SCLK; R39/R40 pull CS high. Loaded levels, ramps and warm-reset active states still need checks. |
| **J3 — CLKSEL self-tied to undriven CLK.** Each ADC CLK37 joined only CLKSEL52; CLK could not define its own reset selection. | **Yes, wiring fixed.** U1 CLKSEL high, U2 low; both CLK37 pins now share `/ADC_CLK`. No clock wire is missing. Firmware must enable U1 CLK_EN and initialize ADC2 after clock readiness; execution is unverified. |

Counts refer to **six distinct wiring arrangements**, not six checklist rows. J3 overlaps several arrangements. No completion percentage across generic conditions is calculated.

Across the 48 checklist rows: **5 FIXED, 24 CANNOT VERIFY, 9 NOT FOUND and 10 NOT APPLICABLE**. There are no confirmed STILL PRESENT, PARTIALLY FIXED or NEW IN V1.4 rows. A1/F1 retain uncertainty because V0's floating voltage is unknown; their underlying H2 wiring fault is nevertheless demonstrably corrected. New power-bank/CS-ramp concerns remain uncertainties, not confirmed faults.
